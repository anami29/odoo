Agreement Management

Template-versioned agreements, sequential Party A → Party B e-signing, executed PDF and supplementary annexures — Odoo 18 Community

  • Templates with Draft → Active → Superseded versions; activation supersedes automatically, one Active version per template.
  • Template-specific dynamic fields (Odoo Properties: text, number, currency, date, boolean, selection, contact) shown under Other Information; mandatory fields block confirmation.
  • Configurable signature locations (multiple areas, multiple pages, per party, required/optional) placed in the content with {{sig:CODE}}.
  • Confirmation assigns the agreement number and locks the template version and content.
  • Sequential signing on touch / stylus devices: Party A completes all required areas before Party B areas unlock.
  • Execution generates and stores the official executed PDF; print/download; automatic or manual email distribution with a full audit log.
  • Supplementary Annexures linked to executed agreements with their own numbering, content, fields, signing, PDF and distribution.
  • Search, filters, grouping, chatter audit trail, security groups and record rules, multi-company.
